- **After a Roon update, `roon_status` tells the truth.** It showed the Core's version and the self-test from when Deep Roon started, also after the Core had updated and restarted. Now Deep Roon notices the new version when it pairs again, and the self-test runs again at the next `roon_status`, since a Roon update is exactly when the internal protocol may have changed.
- **`roon_library` and `roon_focus` take `exclude_producer`.** It's Roon's own "leave out" for producers and engineers: "the ECM albums Manfred Eicher did *not* produce" is now one question instead of reading the credits of every album.
- **`roon_now_playing` knows the disc of a box set.** Since Roon gave box sets a title and cover per disc, the zone feed still shows only the album's main cover. On an album of more than one disc, `roon_now_playing` now adds `disc`: its number, how many there are, Roon's disc title ("Sessions and Demos") and the disc's own cover as `image`. One look at the album per half hour, so it costs nothing per track. Contributed by huytman on GitHub.
- **Search has more patience on a large library.** On a library of 322,000 tracks a search took 9 seconds, and right after a Core update, while Roon rebuilds its index, it ran past the 15 seconds every request got — the self-test then called a working search broken (#4). Roon's heavy library questions (search, Focus, the library's pages) now get 30 seconds; everything else still notices a lost connection in 15. A slow but working read is no longer a failure in the self-test but "works, but slow", and after a Core update the self-test waits a few minutes before it runs again.
