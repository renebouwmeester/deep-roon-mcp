Queue edits that hit the item you meant.

`roon_queue` now gives every row an `item`: the id of that place in the queue, which stays the same while the track is in it. `roon_queue_jump`, `roon_queue_remove` and `roon_queue_move` take it too (`item`, `items`, `to_item`). Positions shift by one every time a track ends, so a remove or move based on a `roon_queue` from a minute ago could touch the neighbour of the track you meant. With the `item`, the tool finds the track wherever it is now, and refuses if it's gone (played or removed) instead of touching another. Without it everything works as before.

`roon_queue_move` also reports where the item really stood.
