A small fix for `roon_status`.

- **The self-test probes with an album from your own library.** It searched for a fixed name ("Miles Davis"), and on a Core with a local library only and no Qobuz or TIDAL that found nothing — `roon_status` then called search and album pages "partly broken" while they worked (#3, thanks @yuubime). It now searches for the most recently added album in the library; an empty library counts as "not tested", not as a failure.
