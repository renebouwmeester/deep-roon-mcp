- **Roon profiles** — the play history, most played, Daily Picks, Daily Mixes, New for you, favourites and Listen Later belong to a Roon profile, and Deep Roon always used the first one; switching profiles in a Roon app doesn't change it, since Deep Roon is a Roon remote of its own. Now `ROON_PROFILE` (or **Roon profile** in Claude Desktop's settings) chooses it by name, and those tools take `profile` for one question. Asked on the forum by dp — thank you!
- `roon_profiles` — the profiles on the Core, and the one Deep Roon uses.
- An empty play history (a new profile) no longer times out: `roon_history` and `roon_top` say there are no plays.
