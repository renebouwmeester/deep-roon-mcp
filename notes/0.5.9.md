- `roon_devices` — Roon's RAAT audio devices as Settings › Audio shows them: per device the machine and address of its RAAT server, whether it's enabled, and whether it has a zone now.
- `roon_device_reset` — disable and enable a device that hangs on "Enabling…" (enabled, but no zone), by name or by the address of its RAAT server; devices in any other state are left alone. After failed connections — a phone whose Roon app restarted or changed networks — the Core waits longer and longer before it tries again; this is the disable + enable you'd do by hand.
