Tags (asked on the forum: "Is there any way to set tags remotely?").

- **`roon_tags`** lists your tags (name, key, and whether it's a smart tag, which Roon fills from its own rules).
- **`roon_album_tags`** gives the tags of one album.
- **`roon_tag_albums`** puts albums into a tag by its name, or takes them out with `remove: true`; `create: true` makes the tag first. Up to 500 albums at a time, so "find every album that's a Target CD and tag it" works: Claude finds the albums with the tools that were already there, shows you the list, and tags them once you agree (`confirm: true` is required). Smart and read-only tags are refused, and every album is read back.
- **`roon_tag_delete`** deletes a tag by its name (the albums stay in your library), also with `confirm: true`.

The two writing tools are hidden with a read-only library (`DEEP_ROON_READ_ONLY`), like the other writes. Like them, they go through Roon's internal protocol — keep a recent backup of your Roon database before you let an assistant change things.
